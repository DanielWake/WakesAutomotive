Our Story

Wakes Autocare, formerly known as ST Vehicle Servicing and Repairs, is a family-established business built on trust, expertise, and a genuine passion for automotive care.

With over 18 years of combined experience in the automotive industry, our team has worked with some of the most prestigious brands including Rolls Royce, BMW, Land Rover, Volkswagen, and Ford.

We're not just technicians - we're time-served professionals who have dedicated our careers to understanding every aspect of vehicle maintenance and repair. This expertise allows us to provide the highest quality service, whether you drive a luxury vehicle or an everyday family car.
