Service Types

Full Service

Recommended annually or every 12,000 miles

  • Engine oil and filter replacement
  • Air filter replacement
  • Cabin/pollen filter replacement
  • Spark plugs (if due)
  • Brake fluid check/replacement (if due)
  • Coolant check/top-up
  • Full vehicle inspection (brakes, suspension, lights, tyres, etc.)
  • Diagnostic check for fault codes
  • Service light reset
  • Service history stamped

Interim Service

Recommended every 6 months or 6,000 miles for high-mileage drivers

  • Engine oil and filter replacement
  • Fluid level checks and top-ups
  • Visual inspection of brakes
  • Tyre condition and pressure check
  • Battery health check
  • Lights and wipers check
  • Basic safety inspection
  • Service light reset
  • Service history recorded
