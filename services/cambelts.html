Critical Engine Protection

A broken cambelt or timing chain can destroy your engine. Regular replacement according to manufacturer schedules is essential to prevent catastrophic engine damage.

We specialize in cambelt (timing belt) and timing chain replacement for all makes and models. Our experienced technicians follow manufacturer procedures and use quality parts to ensure your engine is protected.

Typical cambelt intervals:
• 4-7 years or 40,000-100,000 miles
• Check your service book for specific intervals
• Age matters as much as mileage - rubber perishes over time

Wet Belt Replacement Specialists

Been told to sell your car because of a wet belt? We can help!

Many modern cars (particularly Ford, Vauxhall, and PSA group vehicles) have wet belts - timing belts that run in engine oil. When these fail, some garages recommend scrapping the car. We disagree!

Real customer story: Customer contacted us after being advised to sell their Vauxhall Crossland due to wet belt needing replacement. We replaced: wet belt/timing belt, tensioner, idler, oil & filter, rear discs & pads, sump sealant, and removed the sump to clean any broken belt bits from the strainer. Car saved!
