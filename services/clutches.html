Professional Clutch Replacement

A worn or failing clutch can make driving difficult and damage other components. If you're experiencing clutch problems, we can help.

Our experienced technicians have replaced clutches on everything from small hatchbacks to high-performance sports cars. We use quality parts and ensure the job is done right the first time.

Signs your clutch needs replacing:
• Slipping clutch (engine revs but car doesn't accelerate)
• Heavy or stiff clutch pedal
• Clutch biting point very high or low
• Difficulty selecting gears
• Burning smell
• Vibrations when pressing clutch pedal

Dual Mass Flywheel Replacement

Many modern diesel vehicles and some petrol cars have dual mass flywheels (DMF)

What is a Dual Mass Flywheel?

A dual mass flywheel is a two-piece flywheel designed to reduce vibrations and provide smoother gear changes. While they improve driving comfort, they do wear out over time and can be expensive to replace.

Our Honest Advice

When replacing your clutch, we'll inspect the dual mass flywheel. We'll only recommend replacement if it's genuinely needed - we won't push unnecessary work. If your flywheel is borderline, we'll explain the options and let you decide.
