Our Diagnostic Process

1. Initial Consultation

We discuss the symptoms you're experiencing and any warning lights that have appeared.

2. Diagnostic Scan

We connect professional diagnostic equipment to read fault codes and live data from your vehicle's systems.

3. Analysis

Using our experience and expertise, we interpret the data to identify the root cause of the problem.

4. Testing

We perform physical checks and component tests to confirm the diagnosis.

5. Explanation & Quote

We explain what we've found in plain English and provide a clear quote for any required repairs.

6. Repair & Verify

With your approval, we complete the repair and verify the fault is fixed with a final diagnostic check.
