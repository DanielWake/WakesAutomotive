Pre-MOT Inspection

Avoid MOT failure - get your vehicle checked first

Our comprehensive pre-MOT check identifies potential failures before your test, saving you time and money.

What We Check

  • Lights (all external lights and indicators)
  • Tyres (tread depth, condition, pressure)
  • Brakes (efficiency, condition, fluid)
  • Suspension (shock absorbers, springs, bushes)
  • Steering (play, condition, power steering)
  • Exhaust (leaks, security, emissions)
  • Windscreen & wipers
  • Mirrors
  • Seatbelts
  • Horn
  • Battery & charging system
  • Registration plates

Benefits

Why get a pre-MOT check?

  • Identify issues before they cause failure
  • Fix problems on your schedule, not under pressure
  • Avoid wasted MOT test fees
  • Budget for any necessary repairs
  • Increase chance of first-time pass
  • Peace of mind

We provide a written report of what we find and quote for any work needed.
