Serving the South Coast

Wakes Autocare provides professional car servicing, repairs, and diagnostics across Portsmouth, Waterlooville, Havant, Fareham, and the wider South Coast area.

Workshop Location: Our fully-equipped workshop is based in Waterlooville, Hampshire - centrally located for easy access from Portsmouth, Havant, and surrounding areas.

Mobile Service: Can't get to us? We come to you! Our mobile service covers the entire South Coast, bringing professional automotive care to your home or workplace.

With over 18 years of combined experience and IMI & ATA accreditation, we provide the same high-quality service whether you visit our workshop or use our mobile service.
